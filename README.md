# Puberty -  Help for Teens(on working)

**Welcome to Puberty Help** – your digital buddy through one of life’s biggest changes. Whether you're confused, curious, or just want a safe space to learn, this web app has your back.

---

## Why This App?

Puberty can be weird, awkward, and even a little scary. But guess what? You're not alone. This app is built to make the journey easier with:

- **Straightforward info** without the cringe
- **Cool minimalist design** that feels calm and private
- **Interactive tools** to learn and ask questions

---

## Features

- **Smooth Navigation Bar** – Jump to what matters without the fuss
- **Minimalist Vibes** – Clean design, no clutter
- **Interactive Cards** – Quick actions for learning or reaching out
